# Code of conduct

nextship follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
in every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere someone
represents the project.

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, and criticise the work rather than the
person. Harassment, personal attacks and publishing someone's private information are
not tolerated.

## Reporting

Report a conduct concern privately to the maintainers at gowtham9112006@gmail.com.
Reports are handled confidentially and answered within a week.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits and issues that break this code, and
may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban a participant, following the enforcement
guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
